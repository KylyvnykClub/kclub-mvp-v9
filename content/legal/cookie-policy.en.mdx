## 2. WHAT ARE COOKIES

Cookies are small data files that are stored on a user's device when they visit a website.

Cookies may be used to enable site functionality, enhance security, store user preferences, analytics, statistics, marketing and improve user experience.

The Platform may also use similar technologies, including pixels, tags, SDKs, local browser storage, device identifiers and other tracking technologies.
