## 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

These Partner Rules govern the participation of business partners in the KYLYVNYK CLUB platform, the procedure for posting business profiles, publishing special conditions, interaction with club members and the use of partner functions of the platform.

These Rules are in addition to:

- Terms of Use;

- Privacy Policy;

- Cookie Policy;

- other official documents of KYLYVNYK CLUB.

In the event of a conflict between these Rules and the Terms of Use, the Terms of Use shall prevail, unless otherwise expressly provided in these Rules.

Using the platform’s partner functions means the partner’s full and unconditional agreement with these Rules.

If a partner does not agree with these Rules, he is obliged to stop using the partner functions of the platform.

## 2. WHO CAN BECOME A PARTNER

KYLYVNYK CLUB partners can become:

- legal entities;

- individual entrepreneurs;

- self-employed persons;

- licensed specialists;

- independent consultants;

- professional organizations;

- non-profit organizations;

- state and municipal organizations, if their participation is permitted by applicable law;

- other persons and organizations whose activities comply with the requirements of the platform.

Partner status is provided solely at the discretion of KYLYVNYK CLUB.

Submitting an application to participate as a partner does not create an obligation for the platform to approve such application.

KYLYVNYK CLUB has the right to refuse to grant partner status without giving reasons to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 3. REQUIREMENTS FOR A PARTNER

The partner is obliged:

- carry out activities legally;

- comply with applicable laws;

- provide reliable, current and complete information;

- update information in a timely manner;

- do not mislead users;

- comply with these Rules;

- comply with the Terms of Use of the platform;

- respect the rights of users and other partners;

- act in good faith when interacting with club members;

- comply with published special conditions in accordance with these Rules, Terms of Use and other platform documents.

The partner has no right:

- publish false or misleading information;

- use fraudulent schemes;

- hide the essential terms of offers;

- publish deliberately false special conditions;

- impersonate a representative of KYLYVNYK CLUB;

- use the KYLYVNYK CLUB brand without the permission of the platform;

- violate the rights of third parties;

- carry out activities that could cause damage to the platform, its users or other partners.

The Partner is solely responsible for its activities, products, services, licenses, permits, prices, guarantees, marketing materials and compliance with laws.

## 4. LICENSES AND PERMITS

If a partner’s activities require licenses, permits, certificates or other permits, the partner must have such documents during the entire period of participation in the platform.

At the request of KYLYVNYK CLUB, the partner is obliged to provide documents confirming:

- business registration;

- the right to conduct business;

- availability of licenses;

- availability of permits;

- availability of certificates;

- the right to represent the relevant business, organization or entrepreneur.

The Partner undertakes to immediately notify the platform of the termination of licenses, permits or other documents that may affect the legality of its activities.

Posting a business profile does not mean that KYLYVNYK CLUB has checked, confirmed or guarantees that the partner has all the necessary licenses and permits.

## 5. BUSINESS CHECK

KYLYVNYK CLUB has the right to verify partners and business profiles before publication, after publication, or at any time when using the platform.

The check may include:

- analysis of documents provided by the partner;

- analysis of official registers;

- analysis of licenses and permits;

- website analysis;

- social network analysis;

- analysis of public information;

- analysis of reviews and complaints;

- analysis of information received from platform users;

- other legal verification methods.

KYLYVNYK CLUB has the right to use automated and non-automated methods for verifying information.

Conducting an inspection does not imply approval, recommendation, certification or guarantee of the quality of the partner’s activities.

The platform is not obliged to constantly monitor the activities of partners.

Lack of verification or failure to conduct verification does not mean approval, recommendation, guarantee, certification or acceptance by the platform of responsibility for the activities of the partner, its goods, services, licenses, permits or compliance with the law.

The Partner undertakes to cooperate with the platform in conducting checks and to provide timely information reasonably requested.

Refusal to provide information or documents may result in:

- refusal to publish a profile;

- profile hiding;

- limitation of certain functions;

- suspension of partner status;

- termination of participation in the partner program.

## 7. SPECIAL CONDITIONS OF PARTNERS

The partner has the right to place on the platform special conditions, discounts, bonuses, promotions, privileges or other offers for club members.

Each partner independently determines the content of their proposals and bears full responsibility for their legality, reliability, relevance and execution.

The Partner undertakes not to publish special conditions, discounts, bonuses or other offers that are deliberately false, misleading, discriminatory, illegal or objectively impracticable.

If a partner places, publishes or confirms a special condition on the KYLYVNYK CLUB platform, the partner undertakes to comply with such condition in relation to a club member who:

- has a valid digital club card;

- meets the requirements of the proposal;

- fulfilled the terms of the relevant offer.

The partner does not have the right to arbitrarily refuse to provide a published special condition to a club member if the participant complies with all the requirements of the offer.

If the offer contains restrictions, territorial conditions, validity periods, requirements for pre-registration, minimum order amount, quantitative restrictions or other material conditions, such conditions must be clearly and unambiguously indicated by the partner.

The partner is obliged to promptly update information about special conditions and immediately notify the platform of termination or significant changes to the offer.

Systematic violation of published special conditions may be grounds for the application of measures provided for in these Rules.

Despite the provisions of this section, KYLYVNYK CLUB is not a guarantor of the fulfillment of the partner’s obligations and is not responsible for the partner’s refusal to provide goods, services, discounts, bonuses or other special conditions.
