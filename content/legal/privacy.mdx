# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is critically important to us.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect minimal data necessary to maintain your membership:
- Phone number
- Display name
- Country and Language preferences
- Session metadata (IP, user agent) for security purposes

## 2. GDPR Compliance
We process your data in accordance with the GDPR. We have explicitly decided not to appoint an Article 27 representative as our monitoring scope is limited.

## 3. Data Deletion
You can request account deletion at any time, which moves your account into a `pending_deletion` state. Full erasure occurs after a mandated retention period.
